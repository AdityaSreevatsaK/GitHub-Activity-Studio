How to read the sky

A sky with a memory.

The upper chart preserves all 53 weeks; the lower signal lens magnifies only recorded events, revealing long repository journeys without inventing activity. Spotlight a repository to trace its work. Columns are weeks, rows are Sunday through Saturday. Brighter tiles mean more recorded activity units on that UTC date. A curved line connects distinct dated events from the same repository. Subtle pulses replay those timestamped events.

Unlike GitHub’s official contribution calendar, this demo records only events supplied in the shared fixture. An unlit day does not prove there were zero contributions.
