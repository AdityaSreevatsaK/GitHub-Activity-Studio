How the calendar grows

  • Each tile is one UTC date, arranged in 53 weeks and 7 weekday rows. The same dates and activity underpin both worlds.
  • Source-backed daily event units grow a tile at thresholds 1, 3, 6 and 10. Empty cells mean no recorded event in this input.
  • The archipelago grows sandbars, forests and island workshops. The village grows farms, cottages and towers.
  • Repository markers 1–5 identify actual source repositories on their most active distinct recorded dates. Commit, PR, issue and release symbols remain distinct.
