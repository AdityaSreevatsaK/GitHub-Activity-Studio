Repository neighbourhood

How the landscape grows

  • Every repository starts with a home. Source-backed activity units unlock stages at 1, 3, 6 and 10.
  • Growth 1 lights windows; growth 2 adds gardens and paths; growth 3 upgrades roofs; growth 4 raises pennants.
  • Commit units grow plants (3+). Opened PRs build scaffolding; merged PRs add boats or bridges. Issues add noticeboards; releases add glowing beacons.
  • Unlike events remain distinct, and never become a guessed productivity score.
